Weight this evaluation toward working behaviour, not technical skill. A technical round
has already been scored separately; do not re-score it here.

Score primarily on:
- Specificity. A named, dated, concrete example counts; a general description of how they
  usually work does not.
- Ownership language: whether they can state what they personally decided and did, as
  distinct from what their team did.
- Outcomes. Whether each story reached a result, and whether the result was honest
  including when it went badly.
- Response to conflict and to criticism: did anything actually change afterwards.
- Self-awareness: naming a real weakness with evidence, rather than a strength in disguise.
- Consistency with the resume. Does the career story hang together, and did the candidate
  explain their moves and their own part in the work they listed.

Treat as significant negatives:
- Blaming colleagues, managers or former employers for every failure described.
- A failure story with no consequence and no learning.
- Staying generic after being asked twice for a specific instance.

Do not score the questions the candidate asked at the end of the interview.

Note explicitly in the summary if the candidate avoided a whole area. Conflict and
failure are the two most often deflected, and an avoided area is not a neutral result.

Calibrate to {{experience}}: expect ownership of people or direction from senior
candidates, and ownership of their own work from junior ones.
